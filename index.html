THE 3-MINUTE GUIDE

Small checks. Safer choices.

◷ Three short lessons

You don’t need to be a tech expert. A few calm habits can help you spot pressure tactics and protect your accounts.

↗01 / 03

Pause on links

Scam messages can copy familiar logos and create urgency. A familiar design does not prove a message is genuine.

  • Check the full sender and web address.
  • Don’t use a link in an unexpected urgent message.
  • Open the official app or type a known address yourself.
!Red flag“Act now or your account will close.”
⌑02 / 03

Protect sign-ins

If one password is exposed, reusing it can put other accounts at risk. Add another layer where it’s available.

  • Use a different, long password for each account.
  • Use a password manager or passkey if available.
  • Turn on multi-factor authentication (MFA).
✓Good habitUnique password + MFA for important accounts.
₹03 / 03

Slow down payments

Scammers may impersonate support staff or pressure you to “verify” a payment. Keep payment secrets to yourself.

  • Never share an OTP, UPI PIN or screen with a stranger.
  • You don’t need a UPI PIN or OTP to receive money.
  • Verify payment requests through a trusted channel.
!RememberEntering your UPI PIN authorizes a payment.
